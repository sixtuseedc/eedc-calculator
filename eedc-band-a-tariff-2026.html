EEDC Band A Tariff 2026: ₦225 per kWh

Last updated: June 2026 | Source: NERC MYTO 2026

Band A Quick Facts:
• Tariff: ₦225/kWh
• Minimum Supply: 20+ hours of electricity daily
• Areas: Parts of Onitsha GRA, Nnewi, Awka, Enugu GRA, Abakaliki

How Many Units Will You Get on Band A?

After 7.5% VAT deduction:

  • ₦1,000 = 4.14 units
  • ₦2,000 = 8.27 units
  • ₦5,000 = 20.68 units
  • ₦10,000 = 41.36 units

Is Your Area Band A?

Check your last token receipt. If you see “BAND A” or your rate is ₦225, you’re on Band A. EEDC must give you 20+ hours light daily. If not, report to NERC.
