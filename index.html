3. EEDC Prepaid FAQs 2026

What is EEDC Band A tariff 2026? ₦225 per kWh. You get 20+ hours of light daily.

Why is EEDC deducting money from my recharge? 7.5% VAT is deducted first per NERC. ₦10,000 recharge = ₦750 VAT, ₦9,250 for units.

How to reduce EEDC bill? Switch off AC when not home. Use LED bulbs. Defrost freezer. Moving from Band A to Band C saves 70%.
